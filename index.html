Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shopping with Black Bird.

How does Cash on Delivery (COD) work? +
With Cash on Delivery, you don't need any bank cards or advance deposits. You place your order online, we process and dispatch it, and you hand over the cash amount to our delivery rider when they reach your doorstep.
What are the delivery charges and delivery times? +
We charge a flat delivery fee of Rs 300 for all orders across the Vavuniya District. Deliveries are typically completed within 24 to 48 hours after order confirmation.
Which areas in Vavuniya do you deliver to? +
We deliver to all major areas including Vavuniya Town, Nedunkeni, Omanthai, Puliyankulam, Cheddikulam, Iranamadu, Kovilkulam, Periyakulam, and surrounding neighborhoods.
Can I place orders or ask questions directly on WhatsApp? +
Yes! You can contact us anytime on WhatsApp at +94 70 751 7768. Our team can help you check product availability, answer questions, or take orders directly.
